Ongoing Research 2026

Encoder-Aware Visual Token Compression for Efficient VLMs

Moneeba Abrar

Studying how visual token compression behaves across different vision encoders (ViT, DINOv2, SigLIP, ConvNeXt-V2). Benchmarking a random baseline and Top-K pruning against an adaptive compression method.

  • Contextual token selection works well on ViT but fails on DINOv2 / SigLIP
  • Shows compression effectiveness is encoder-dependent, not universal
